Method & caveats

How these numbers are made.

Hands-on hours are a model, not a measurement: each prompt I typed counts as the 15 minutes after it, and a day's hours are the union of those windows. Where windows from two projects overlap, the time is split evenly, so the projects in a day add up to that day's total. Long stretches of reading, testing, or thinking between prompts are undercounted; scattered prompts are overcounted.

Sources. Claude Code and Codex sessions from my two Macs, cloud sessions, and claude.ai chats, which I archive. Cloud sessions are found only through commits on a default branch, so one that made no commits is missing. claude.ai chats are as recent as the last data export: —. ChatGPT is not covered.

Colours follow the project. A project keeps its colour on every chart, chip, and log row; with more projects than colours, two projects share a colour only when they never appear in the same view.

Projects are named when they are public repos, private repos I chose to include, or work off GitHub I chose to name. Everything else is "Other". Session titles and prompts are never published. Commits come from GitHub, and from the local history of a repo off GitHub that I chose to name; those have no link.

Cost is in each bar's tooltip. For Claude and Codex it is API-equivalent: the session's tokens priced at list rates. It is not what a subscription charges, and Claude's cache writes are priced at the cheaper 5-minute rate. Modal is the real bill.

Time is local, from my Swarm check-ins; before the first check-in, New York. Days away show the city or country, never more.
