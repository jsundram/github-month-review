      Method & caveats

      What this page knows—and what it doesn't.

      Weeks are Monday-based and clipped to the month, so the first and last week of a month are often short. A week that straddles a month boundary appears in both, split.

      The source is public repository activity. Private repos and non-GitHub work are absent, and a quiet week here does not mean a quiet week in general.

      AI-assisted and coauthored commits are counted as work directed, reviewed, and shipped by the repo owner — not as a claim about who typed each line. Travel and personal context is supplied by hand; none of it is inferred from commit metadata.
